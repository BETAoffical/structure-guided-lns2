# STRIDE SafeSlot Gate v1 Preregistration

## Question

This stage asks whether pre-action information can identify when one of the six
frozen SlotPool structural candidates should replace the exact base-only V2
anchor. It trains a preventive, abstaining gate. It does not change candidate
generation, rank the original and structural pools together, run a solver, or
measure TTF.

The development cohort is frozen before training: 98 states from 16 maps, 588
SlotPool candidates, and 201 final residual-qualified positives. The single
candidate whose agent set exactly equals the V2 anchor is removed by an
outcome-blind identity rule, leaving 587 eligible challenger rows. No map,
state, or candidate is removed based on its label.

## Pre-action representation

The model input has 193 fields:

1. 124 realized-feature differences, challenger minus exact V2 anchor;
2. 46 challenger-only SlotPool descriptors covering family, size, provenance,
   V2 overlap, support, and size-to-support ratios;
3. 23 shared state fields from the realized-feature schema.

The residual teacher, PP outcome, TTF, future trajectory, Cost-to-Go, and
remaining repair rounds are labels or forbidden data, never inputs. Current
no-progress streak and overlap with a recent failed neighborhood are also
excluded: the available source trajectories were generated by other
controllers and do not provide matched SafeSlot runtime history. They may be
studied only after a separate matched-data registration.

Each state has total training weight one, divided uniformly over its eligible
challengers. This prevents states with six rows from dominating the one state
with five rows.

## Nested map-grouped validation

The outer evaluation uses four whole-map folds. Hyperparameters and the action
threshold are selected only inside each outer training set using three inner
whole-map folds. A map and all its states remain in exactly one validation fold
at a time.

For every state, the gate chooses the highest-probability challenger only when
its score reaches the inner-selected threshold; otherwise it executes the
exact V2 anchor. Thresholds are considered in the fixed order 0.70 through
0.95. The 1.01 sentinel means full abstention and is an explicit calibration
failure, not a successful safe model.

An inner threshold must achieve at least 80% safe-replacement precision, act on
at least 10% of states and half of the available maps, and remain noninferior
to V2 in mean current-step, both fixed seed halves, no-progress rate, and mean
residual risk. Per-map current-step regret and residual-risk degradation may
not exceed 0.02.

## Offline decision

Promotion to fresh-map label confirmation requires all four outer calibrations
to be feasible, candidate ROC-AUC of at least 0.65, weighted accuracy at least
0.03 above the majority baseline, selected-action precision of at least 80%,
at least 10% state coverage and eight selected maps, and every registered
current-step/residual noninferiority gate.

If any gate fails, no deployment artifact is promoted and the same labels may
not be repeatedly tuned. If all gates pass, the model is still offline-only.
The next step must collect the same labels on development-external maps before
runtime integration. Solver success, the known Maze regression, repair rounds,
overhead, and raw TTF remain separate later gates.
